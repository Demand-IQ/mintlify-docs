AI Presentations by Demand IQ turns a reusable template into a narrated, interactive presentation for each prospect.

A **template** holds your slides, narration, FAQs, and branding. The API calls that same object a **deck**. A **presentation** is a personalized snapshot of a template, created for one prospect and shared through its own link.

## Key capabilities

### Build

- **Templates and branding:** create structured or image-based templates with your colors, fonts, and logo.
- **AI generation:** let AI extract slide content, write narration scripts, and generate FAQs.
- **Knowledge base:** upload company documents to inform answers about your products and services.

### Deliver

- **Personalized links:** create a presentation for each prospect with their name, address, and pricing inputs.
- **Email and notifications:** send presentation links by email and track delivery and opens through webhooks or email notifications.
- **Appointment scheduling:** let viewers book appointments from the presentation.

### Engage

- **Narration:** play spoken slide content with synchronized text highlighting.
- **Live Q&A:** answer viewer questions by voice or text using FAQs and AI-generated responses.
- **Voice commands:** let viewers navigate, control audio, and take action by voice or text.

### Close

- **Product pricing:** configure product tiers and pricing formulas, with automated roof measurements to resolve measurement-based prices.
- **Journeys:** populate product slides from live journey data. See [Journey-backed product slides](/ai-presentations/guides/deck-types#journey-backed-product-slides) for tier mapping.
- **Contracts and e-signatures:** configure contract templates and collect typed signatures during the presentation.
